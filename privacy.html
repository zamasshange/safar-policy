Privacy policy

This policy explains how Safar handles personal information when you use the Safar Quran app or this website, and how to contact us about it.

Accounts

When you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier and sign-in information to manage your access. If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles its sign-in flow. Safar can use your display name to personalise the app.

Reading and device access

  • Safar stores reading progress, saved ayat, settings and private reflections on your device. Natural Search looks up verses using an index on your device.
  • Recitation Check uses the microphone and your device’s speech recognition service when you start a check and grant permission. Depending on the device and settings, the speech provider may process the audio. Safar does not send Recitation Check audio to its AI service.
  • The Qibla compass uses orientation sensors to show direction. On iPhone, the system may use approximate location, when permitted, to correct for true north.
  • You can change microphone, speech recognition, location and notification permissions in your device settings. When you choose to share a verse card, your chosen app handles the shared content.

Online content and AI services

Safar requests some Quran content and audio from external providers, including Quran.com. Those requests can expose technical information such as your IP address to the provider.

When you use an available AI feature such as Chat or Explain, Safar sends your question and any relevant conversation or verse text to its AI service to generate a response. The service may forward that content to OpenRouter and the model provider. Avoid including personal or sensitive details in your questions. If you report an AI response, Safar stores the selected response, its source screen and the report time so it can be reviewed.

External service providers may process information outside South Africa.

Subscriptions

Apple or Google processes payment through the relevant app store. Safar uses RevenueCat to associate your Safar account identifier with purchases and subscription status so it can unlock or restore access. Safar does not receive your card number.

Website and support

This website’s reader and feature tour run in your browser. The site code does not set cookies or include advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard connection details, such as your IP address, to serve and secure the site.

If you call or email us, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond and handle your request. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Retention and your rights

Account information is used while you maintain an account. Local reading data can be removed by clearing the app’s data. Service providers may retain records for billing, security or legal obligations. Contact us about retention or deletion of your support messages, reported responses or other information.

You can delete your Safar account or request deletion by email. Where applicable, you may also request access to or correction of your personal information, object to its processing, or withdraw consent where processing depends on it. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

In South Africa, you can lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

We’ll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
