Privacy policy

Delete your Safar account

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Safar and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete account under Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion. Safar may ask you to sign in again to verify the request.

Request deletion by email

Email a Safar account deletion request. Include the email address associated with your Safar account so we can verify your request. Keep your password private.

What deletion covers

Account deletion removes your Safar sign-in account and profile. Reading notes and history stored on your device can be removed by clearing the app’s data. Contact support about other information associated with your account. Some transaction records may be retained for legal, security or billing purposes.

To stop a subscription from renewing, cancel it separately in the store where you subscribed.
